The best local LLM for a 256GB Mac

M3 Ultra 256GB — about 240.0 GiB usable once macOS has taken its share.

Unified memory is the whole game on Apple Silicon. Your 256GB is shared between macOS, your apps and the model, so the honest budget is closer to 240.0 GiB than to 256. Everything below is sized against that number, at Q4_K_M with an 8K context.

The picks

Best all-round: DeepSeek V4 Flash Vision Exp

The largest general-purpose model that still leaves room to work. It loads in 183.9 GiB and generates around 4 tokens/sec on an M3 Ultra 256GB. Full breakdown →

Best for coding: Qwen2.5-Coder 32B

Trained specifically on code, and worth the swap if that is your workload. It loads in 22.4 GiB and generates around 33 tokens/sec on an M3 Ultra 256GB. Full breakdown →

Best for reasoning: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B

Thinks before answering; slower per question, better on hard ones. It loads in 45.6 GiB and generates around 15 tokens/sec on an M3 Ultra 256GB. Full breakdown →

Fastest usable: Llama 3.2 1B

When latency matters more than depth — voice assistants, autocomplete, agents. It loads in 1.8 GiB and generates around 863 tokens/sec on an M3 Ultra 256GB. Full breakdown →

A model that is a gigabyte or two over can often be rescued by dropping to Q3_K_M or quantising the KV cache. Anything further over than that is better solved by picking a smaller model — a 14B at Q4 beats a 32B at Q2 on almost every task.

Somewhere to keep the weights

Model files are large and Apple's internal storage is expensive. A fast external SSD over Thunderbolt loads weights quickly enough that you will not notice the difference.

  • Thunderbolt / USB4 external SSD
  • Samsung T9 portable SSD
